# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** April 2026

## Overview

Source is a local music player. This application does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

## Permissions

### Audio Files Access

The app requests access to audio files stored on your device in order to provide music playback functionality. These files are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with any external service.

### Network Access

Network access is used exclusively for:

- **Last.fm Integration** (optional): If you choose to enable Last.fm scrobbling in Settings, the app communicates with Last.fm's servers to submit your listening history and display "Now Playing" status.
- **Sonos & Chromecast**: The app uses your local Wi-Fi network to discover and stream audio to Sonos speakers and Chromecast devices. No data is sent to external servers for this purpose.

### Bluetooth & Audio Devices

The app detects connected audio devices (Bluetooth, wired headphones, USB audio) to provide audio output routing. No data about your devices is collected or shared.

### Notifications

On Android 13+, the app requests notification permission to display playback controls in the notification shade.

## Last.fm Integration

If you enable Last.fm scrobbling:

- Your Last.fm username and session key are stored locally on your device.
- Your listening history (artist, track, album, timestamp) is sent to Last.fm when you listen to a song.
- You can disable scrobbling or log out at any time in Settings.
- No Last.fm data is shared with any party other than Last.fm's own servers.

## Sonos & Chromecast

Device discovery uses local network multicast (SSDP/UPnP). Audio is streamed over your local network only. No identifying information is sent to external services.

## Data Storage

All data used by the application (library metadata, playlists, preferences) remains locally on your device. No cloud backups are performed by the app.

## Data Sharing

No data is shared with third parties, except as described in the Last.fm section above when you explicitly enable that feature.
