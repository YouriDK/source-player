- Short description : A stunning, elegant, and powerful local music player designed for your aesthetic

- Long description : Source is the premium music player your local audio library deserves. Crafted with a focus on stunning aesthetics, buttery-smooth animations, and total control, Source makes listening to your offline music an absolute joy.

Whether you meticulously organize your music in folders or rely on perfect metadata tags, Source adapts to your listening style. No ads, no bloated streaming features—just you and your music, beautifully presented.

✨ Key Features:

🎨 Stunning, Customizable Design Experience a truly modern interface that feels alive. Personalize your player with a striking Dark Mode, dynamic accent color pickers, and beautiful typography. Every screen is designed to make your album art pop and your library shine.

📁 Total Organization & Folder Browsing Navigate your music exactly how you want to. Browse your library intuitively by Songs, Albums, Artists, and Genres, or dive directly into your device’s filesystem with seamless Folder Navigation.

🎶 Premium Audio Experience Enjoy flawless, high-quality audio playback. With precise seeking, seamless gapless transitions, and a meticulously engineered audio engine, your favorite tracks have never sounded better.

🏷️ Built-in Tag Editor Fix messy track details on the fly. Edit song titles, artists, albums, and more directly within the app so your library is always perfectly organized.

🔄 Last.fm Integration Keep track of everything you listen to. Source seamlessly connects to your Last.fm account to automatically scrobble your listening history in the background.

🎧 Smart Playlists & Queue Management Create the perfect vibe for any moment. Build custom playlists, manage your dynamic "Up Next" queue effortlessly, and search across your entire library in milliseconds.

Take control of your local audio with Source—where beautiful design meets powerful playback.
